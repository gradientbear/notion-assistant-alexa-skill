# Best Template Sentences for Users

This document provides the best, most user-friendly template sentences for all task management operations. These are optimized for natural conversation and ease of use.

## Quick Reference Guide

### 🎯 CREATE TASKS

**Simplest (Recommended):**
- "add [task name]"
- Example: "add buy milk"

**With Date:**
- "add [task name] [date]"
- Examples:
  - "add buy milk tomorrow"
  - "add call dentist Friday"
  - "add finish report next week"

**With Priority:**
- "add [task name] [priority] priority"
- Examples:
  - "add buy milk high priority"
  - "add call dentist low priority"

**Complete:**
- "add [task name] [priority] priority [date] [category]"
- Example: "add finish report high priority tomorrow work"

---

### 📖 READ TASKS

**All Tasks:**
- "show my tasks"
- "read my tasks"
- "list my tasks"

**By Status:**
- "show my [status] tasks"
- Examples:
  - "show my done tasks"
  - "show my to do tasks"
  - "show my in process tasks"

**By Priority:**
- "show my [priority] priority tasks"
- Examples:
  - "show my high priority tasks"
  - "show my low priority tasks"

**By Category:**
- "show my [category] tasks"
- Examples:
  - "show my work tasks"
  - "show my personal tasks"

**By Date:**
- "show tasks due [date]"
- Examples:
  - "show tasks due today"
  - "show tasks due tomorrow"
  - "show tasks due Friday"

**Combined:**
- "show my [priority] priority [category] tasks"
- Example: "show my high priority work tasks"

---

### ✏️ UPDATE TASKS

**Update Status:**
- "mark [task name] as [status]"

## Best Practices for Users

### ✅ DO:
1. **Be specific with task names:** "buy milk" is better than "milk"
2. **Use natural language:** "tomorrow" instead of "2024-12-25"
3. **Combine information when possible:** "add buy milk high priority tomorrow"
4. **Use simple commands:** "show my tasks" is better than "please display all of my tasks"

### ❌ DON'T:
1. **Don't be too vague:** Avoid "add task" without a name
2. **Don't use complex sentences:** Keep it simple and direct
3. **Don't include unnecessary words:** "add buy milk" not "I would like to add a task to buy milk"

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Task Names:** Use descriptive names that are easy to remember and match
2. **Dates:** Use natural language like "tomorrow", "Friday", "next week"
3. **Priority:** Use "high", "normal", or "low"
4. **Category:** Use "work" or "personal"
5. **Status:** Use "to do", "in process", or "done"
